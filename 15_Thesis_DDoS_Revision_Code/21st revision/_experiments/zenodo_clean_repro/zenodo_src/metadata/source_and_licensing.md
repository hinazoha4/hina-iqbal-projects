# Sources

CICIoT2023 confusion counts and CICDDoS2019 comparison metrics: the executed notebooks behind Iqbal, H. (2026). Bayesian-optimized hybrid deep learning framework for detecting DDoS attacks on MEC and IoT networks. Master’s dissertation, University of Coimbra.

CICDDoS2019 and CICIoT2023: Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity, University of New Brunswick. Obtain them from the Institute. They are not redistributed here.

This deposit rebuilds the prevalence and cost analysis from the printed CICIoT2023 confusion counts and from CICDDoS2019 counts recovered from the printed precision, recall, support, and Matthews correlation. It does not contain raw traffic, model checkpoints, or prediction scores, and it does not retrain the models. The executed notebooks contain private download identifiers and are not included.

BoT-IoT aggregate results are in metadata/botiot_results.json. They are a 350,000-row subsample of a Kaggle mirror, not the earlier truncated-file run. See metadata/botiot_source.md. Raw BoT-IoT files are not redistributed.
The interim public deposit of the historical calculations is https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23435601.
