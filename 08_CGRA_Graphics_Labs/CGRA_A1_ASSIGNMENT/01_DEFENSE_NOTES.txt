============================================================
CGRA A1 — DEFENSE (oral) — bilkul simple answers
============================================================

Professor zero de sakta hai agar explain na kar sako.
Neeche HAR cheez 1–2 jumlon mein yaad rakho.


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Code kis layout pe hai?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: Professor ke Objects.ipynb jaisa.
   Libraries cell → vertex shader → fragment shader → myapp.
   Har shape CGRAobject extend karti hai.
   drawit(view, proj) se MVP = P * V * M banata hai.


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Cube kyun 36 vertices, 8 nahi?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: Geometry mein 8 corners hain, lekin har face ke liye
   alag color/normal chahiye. Is liye har triangle apne
   3 vertices duplicate karta hai: 6 faces × 2 tris × 3 = 36.


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Color gradient kaise?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: Constructor mein 2 colors.
   Har vertex pe t = (x+1)/2
   color = (1-t)*color1 + t*color2
   Matlab left se right horizontal blend.


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Sphere / cylinder / cone / disc?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: Disc  = center + circle points → triangles
   Cone  = apex + base circle triangles
   Cylinder = top+bottom circle, beech mein quads (2 tris)
   Sphere = latitude rings (stacks), har ring pe slices,
            rings ke beech triangles (jaise cylinder)


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Convoy hierarchy?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: Pehle world matrix = Translate(path) * RotateY(yaw)
   Phir body/wheels is matrix ke relative (local offset)
   Parent transform children pe cascade (multiply).


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Key r pe wheels?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: wheelMode = 0,1,2
   Same position pe alag shape draw:
   0=Cube, 1=Cylinder, 2=Sphere + thoda scale/rotate


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Figure-eight path?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: Lemniscate of Bernoulli (PDF formula).
   omega = eps*t + theta_i
   x,z = formula
   Wagon spacing = alag theta_i (negative offsets)


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Orientation / rotation matrix?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: Path ka tangent (dx/dt, dz/dt) nikalta hoon.
   yaw = atan2(dx, dz)
   R = rotation around Y by yaw
   Is se gaadi hamesha path ki direction face karti hai.


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: WASD camera?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: eye position + yaw angle.
   forward vector from yaw.
   W/S along forward, A/D along right.
   lookAt(eye, eye+forward, up)


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Key v?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: camMode toggle.
   0 = free walk
   1 = camera last wagon pe baith kar aage dekhti hai


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Trebuchet / mill animation?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: Trebuchet arm: angle = 40*sin(t)  (pendulum)
   Mill wheel: continuous rotate t*80
   Bucket: y = 0.4 + 0.35*sin(2t)


------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Third-party geometry loader?
------------------------------------------------------------
A: Nahi. Sab vertices mere loops / lists se.
   Sirf professor ki deec* + cgraobject + glm-js.
